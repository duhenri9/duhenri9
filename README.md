## What I bring to an engineering team

- **LLM workflow orchestration:** separate model generation, critique, adversarial evaluation and acceptance instead of asking a single model to certify itself.
- **Agentic architecture:** constrained tool/MCP access, identity and tenancy boundaries, approval gates and explicit outcome checks.
- **Engineering delivery:** translate ambiguous requests into scoped work, reproducible tests, reviewable changes and evidence-linked decisions.
- **Product-to-code translation:** customer onboarding, B2B SaaS workflows, feedback loops and product operations informed by marketplace experience.

I distinguish **designed**, **implemented**, **fixture-tested**, **independently validated** and **production-verified**. This is a reporting standard, not a claim that every project has reached every stage.

### How to review my work in 90 seconds

1. Choose a **product case study** for the business problem, scope and current maturity.
2. Look at **RepoOps**, **ActionSeam** or **WM3 Request Protocol** for executable engineering patterns.
3. Follow the evidence and negative controls; check precisely **what each test proves** and what it does not.

## Working principles

**Explicit boundaries · Independent verification · Small accepted increments · Reproducible evidence · Honest maturity claims**

Public case studies do not reveal private prompts, internal evaluation corpora, customer materials or commercial terms that are still under review.
