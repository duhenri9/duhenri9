## Engineering principles

- executable evidence before broad claims;
- negative controls and fail-closed states;
- explicit authority and scope boundaries;
- reproducible identities, digests and provenance;
- separation of planning, execution, verification and acceptance;
- public claims bounded to the exact mechanism actually tested.

Most repositories are intentionally experimental and document what they **do not** prove as carefully as what they do.
